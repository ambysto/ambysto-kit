Refund Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

In short: A Pro license is a one-time payment. It never renews, so there is nothing to cancel. Ask within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. Refunds arrive in 5–10 business days.

This policy applies to Pro license keys for the Ambysto Pro Suite apps bought from Ambysto Technologies LLC, whether for one app or for the whole suite. It follows the Ambysto Refund & Cancellation Policy, which covers all our products. Where this page is more specific about the Pro Suite, this page applies. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Nothing to cancel

A Pro license is paid once. There is no subscription and no auto-renewal, and you are never charged again for it. The free version of each app stays free.

2. Refunds

Full refund if you request it within 14 days of purchase. Each app has a free version, so you can try it before you buy. After a refund, the license key becomes void and you must stop using it.

3. When we always refund

  • You were charged twice or charged an incorrect amount.
  • We could not deliver the license you paid for.
  • An app update removes a material Pro feature. We refund the license in full when you ask within 30 days of that update.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. The time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your bank or card issuer.

5. Contact

For any billing question, email contact@ambysto.com with your purchase email and your invoice number. We reply within one business day.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

In short: The apps run on your computer. There is no account and no telemetry. Your license key is checked offline. We keep only what we need to sell you a license and answer your email. This website has no cookies and no analytics.

This policy explains how Ambysto Technologies LLC ("Ambysto", "we", "us") handles personal information for the Ambysto Pro Suite apps (DevBox, Optimer, Lean, Power, Tune, Steady and Gate) and this website, suite.ambysto.com. It applies together with the Ambysto Privacy Policy, which covers all our products. Where this page is more specific about the Pro Suite, this page applies.

1. What stays on your computer

The apps run on your computer. There is no account to create and no telemetry. What an app measures, stores or changes stays on your computer, and we do not receive it.

2. Connections the apps make

Each app's own page lists the connections that app makes.

3. Your license key

A Pro license key is checked on your computer, offline. Checking it sends nothing to us.

4. When you buy Pro

Payments are processed by our payment provider. We receive the details needed to issue and support your license, such as your name, email address and billing country, and limited card details such as the card brand, last four digits and expiry date. We do not store full card numbers. We use these details to deliver your key, send your receipt, handle refunds and answer support requests.

5. This website

suite.ambysto.com sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. It loads its fonts and images from its own server, with no third-party requests. It is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver and secure the site.

6. When you email us

If you write to us, we keep your message and email address to answer you and to keep a record of support requests.

7. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run sales and support for the apps, such as payment processing, email delivery and hosting, under contracts that limit their use of the data; with authorities, where required by law or to protect rights and safety; and with a buyer or successor, if our business is involved in a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy.

8. Data retention

We keep purchase and support records as long as needed to support your license, and longer only where we must keep records for legal, tax or accounting reasons.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email contact@ambysto.com. We respond within 30 days. Data that an app keeps on your computer is under your control.

10. International transfers

Ambysto is based in the United States and our providers may process data in other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Children

The apps and this website are not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

12. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page. For material changes, we will notify customers by email.

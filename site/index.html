What we won’t do.

The suite is defined as much by what it refuses as by what it does.

  • Phone homeYour key is checked on your machine, offline. There is no telemetry.
  • Run as rootNot unless it is unavoidable, and then the app says so and asks first.
  • Scan for malwareThat is not what we do. Nor do we update your drivers or your apps.
  • Act before measuringEvery app shows you the numbers before it changes anything.
  • Make a change you cannot undoChanges are reversible, and the app keeps the way back.
  • Sell a “boost”No “Free RAM” button, no health score, no force-quit.
  • Rush youNo countdown, no fake discount, no subscription that renews by itself.
  • Hold features hostageWe only charge when you are happy to pay. Essential cleanup and recovery are free forever.
  • Sell you an adA suggestion appears only when it solves something the app just measured. It is never an ad.

Questions.

Does it need the internet to work?

No. Your license is a signed key file that is checked on your machine.

Does it need admin rights?

Not unless it is unavoidable. If an action needs them, the app says so and asks first.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. Each app measures first and keeps the way back.

Can I buy just one app?

Yes. Each app sells on its own. The suite is one key for all seven.

Does Pro lock the cleanup, the delete, or the restore?

No. Free keeps the job. Pro adds a longer history, export, alerts, and presets.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes. Every app is native on Mac and on Windows, built from the same written spec.
